    Steel (EC3)¶

    The design of steel bars to EN 1993-1-1:2005, with Design ▸ Steel members (ULS). Before you start, see Materials and sections for design (the section must be of type Steel and a supported shape), Design codes and preferences for the parameters, and Buckling lengths for the K factors the stability checks use.

    What is checked¶

    For every steel bar, for each selected ULS combination:

    • the cross-section resistance (§6.2): N, My, Mz, Vy, Vz and T, reported as three ratios: bending (the N + M interaction), shear and torsion;
    • the member stability (§6.3.3): flexural buckling and lateral-torsional buckling together, computed once per physical member (a chain of collinear bars, found automatically) and shared by its bars.

    The governing ratio of each bar is its combined ratio. The forces of each bar are enveloped over its critical points and over the combinations, which is conservative. The axial force is tension-positive, so the design compression is −N.

    The shapes designed are I, rectangular hollow (RHS, SHS) and circular hollow (CHS), normally from a catalogue profile. The in-plane actions are resolved onto the principal axes of the profile with its angle θ (see Materials and sections for design). A section of any other shape is not designed.

    In a plate model¶

    A grillage member has no axial force: the N slot of its end forces is the St-Venant torsion T, and it carries bending out of the plane and shear. Only lateral-torsional buckling is then relevant.

    Choices that change the result¶

    These are in Design ▸ Code preferences ▸ EC3 · Steel:

    Choice Meaning
    Interaction factors kij (§6.3.3) Annex B (method 2, the default) or Annex A (method 1)
    Class 4 effective properties on: a Class 4 section is checked with its effective properties
    LTB rolled/welded method (§6.3.2.3) on: the more favourable method for rolled and welded sections
    γM0, γM1, γM2 the partial factors (1.0, 1.0, 1.25)
    Cmy, Cmz, CmLT, C1 the default equivalent-moment and LTB-moment factors
    K_y, K_z, K_LT the default buckling-length factors, overridden per member in Buckling lengths

    Two rules of the design: hollow sections are not susceptible to lateral-torsional buckling, and the shear area of RHS and SHS sections is the one of the standard. A member can also be marked as not susceptible to lateral-torsional buckling in the Buckling panel.

    The model is 2D

    The out-of-plane bracing is invisible to a planar model. The buckling length out of the plane is the in-plane one unless you set K_z for the member (see Buckling lengths).

    Reading the results¶

    The Design panel lists, under Steel, one row per physical member and combination:

    Column Meaning
    Member, Elements, Section, Profile, L the member, its bars, its section and profile, its length
    Combination, Class the combination, and the cross-section class
    N_Ed, My_Ed, Mz_Ed the design actions
    χy, χz, χLT the reduction factors of buckling about each axis and of lateral-torsional buckling
    6.61, 6.62 the utilisation of the two interaction expressions of §6.3.3
    Check the governing ratio and its verdict

    On the canvas, the Steel design view colours each bar by its utilisation, with a selector for the check shown: combined, bending, shear, torsion or buckling (§6.3).

    The step-by-step calculation of a member is in the design report.
